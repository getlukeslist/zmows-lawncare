Privacy Policy

How we handle your information

Z-Mows Lawn Care is Joe Zmolik's lawn and yard business in Mabank, Texas. This page explains what our website collects and what happens to it.

What the quote form collects

When you ask for a free quote, the form asks for your name, phone number, email, the service you need, and a message about the job.

How we use it

We use what you send only to answer your quote request.

Your request is emailed to Joe through Resend, an email delivery service. Resend only handles the message so it reaches his inbox.

A copy of each quote request also goes to Luke's List Business Solutions, who built and maintains this website, for backup and support.

We never sell your information or share it for advertising.

Website visits

This site uses Google Analytics, which uses cookies, to count visits and taps on our phone number. That helps us see how people find us.

When you send a quote request, Google Analytics also records which service you picked. Your name, phone number, email, and message are never sent to Google Analytics.

Deleting your information

Your request stays in Joe's email and in the Luke's List copy. Ask and we'll delete it from both. Call 972-762-1962 or email zmowslawncare@gmail.com.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy, call Joe at 972-762-1962 or email zmowslawncare@gmail.com.
